# Dear-Data-100
Main Question: How can I take 100 real-world observations of bags people wear during my commute and at university and turn them into a visual artwork using code?

Project Overview
For this project, I am tracking the different bags people carry in their day-to-day lives. Inspired by the Dear Data project, I am gathering 100 total observations and using Processing to create a visual sketch that shows trends, popular brands, and differences in style.

Project Goals
- Collect 100 observations of bags in everyday settings.
- Record key details for each bag: date, bag type, brand, size, color, gender, and location.
- Find patterns in the data (like which brands or bag types are most common in different places).
- Design a creative visual sketch using Processing to display the data.

How I Collect the Data
I collect data points during my daily routine such as on the train and at university. Each observation is initially written down in my notes app on my phone and then later saved in a simple spreadsheet file (data.csv) using these columns:
Date: When I saw the bag.
Type: Handbag (H) or Backpack (B).
Brand: e.g., Longchamp, Coach, Nike, Adidas, Polo, Charles & Keith, North Face.
Size: Small (S), Medium (M), or Large (L).
Color: e.g., Black, Brown, Blue, Burgundy, Cream.
Gender: Female (F) or Male (M).
Location: University (uni) or on my commute (train).

Data Analysis
After collecting the full dataset, I will compare the categories to see how people's choices change depending on where they are. For example, I'll look at whether backpacks are more common on trains while handbags appear more on campus. These findings will help me decide how to design the visual sketch.

Visualizing in Processing
Using Processing, I will transform the data table into a visual artwork:
Shapes: Different shapes will represent handbags vs. backpacks.
Colors: Shapes will match the actual color or brand of the bag observed.
Layout: Points will be organized by date and location.

This project turns plain spreadsheet rows into an interesting art piece about everyday fashion and habits.
